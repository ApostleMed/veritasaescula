IGCSE O Level to Medical School: Your Complete Pathway

IGCSE O Level alone is rarely enough for medical school entry — most universities require a full secondary diploma. Veritas Aescula Institute provides the complete IGCSE + OSSD bridge pathway that makes Southeast Asian students fully qualified applicants.

1

Complete IGCSE O Level at Veritas Aescula

Enroll in our healthcare-specialized IGCSE O Level program. Our curriculum focuses on Biology, Chemistry, Combined Sciences, and medical foundations — building the strong subject grades required as a foundation for medical school entry worldwide.

2

Bridge with OSSD — Your Full Secondary Diploma

This is the critical step most students miss. IGCSE O Level alone does not satisfy the secondary school diploma requirement at most medical universities. Our OSSD (Ontario Secondary School Diploma) bridge program gives you a complete, internationally recognized secondary credential on top of your O Level grades — making you a fully qualified applicant.

3

Build Clinical Hours & Apply to Medical Universities

While completing your OSSD, accumulate structured clinical volunteer hours to strengthen your application. Our admissions counselors then guide you through applying to medical universities in Czech Republic, Hungary, Bulgaria, Poland, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand, Japan, China, UK, Canada, and Australia — with a complete, qualifying application package.

4

Begin Your Medical Career

Join our alumni network of Southeast Asian students now studying medicine at universities across Europe and Asia. With IGCSE O Level + OSSD from Veritas Aescula, you meet the full entry requirements — giving you the strongest possible foundation for a medical degree.

ALMA — A Levels for Med Aspirants

What is ALMA at Veritas Aescula Institute?

ALMA (A Levels for Med Aspirants) is an 18-month, fully online, fast-track programme in Pearson Edexcel International A Level Biology, Chemistry and Mathematics. It is designed for students who want to study medicine. It runs from January 2027 to June 2028.

Is ALMA the full A Level, or a shortened version?

It is the full qualification. Students study every unit of the Pearson Edexcel International Advanced Level (IAL) specification in all three subjects and sit the same official Pearson exams as IAL candidates worldwide. ALMA compresses the calendar, not the content.

How can A Levels be completed in 18 months without losing rigour?

ALMA runs continuously instead of across two school years with long breaks. Students have intensive live classes, weekly exam-style practice, timed topic tests and full mock exams, and every unit is taught in full. Pearson recommends 360 guided learning hours per subject, and the 18-month schedule is built around that.

Which subjects and units does ALMA cover?

ALMA covers Biology (6 units), Chemistry (6 units) and Mathematics (Pure Mathematics 1–4, Statistics 1 and Mechanics 1). In each subject, the first three units form the IAS stage, taught January–December 2027, and the next three form the IA2 stage, taught January–June 2028.

Why Biology, Chemistry and Mathematics?

Biology and Chemistry are the core subjects most medical schools ask for. Mathematics builds the quantitative and statistical skills used in evidence-based medicine and research. A few universities ask for a different combination, such as Physics, so our admissions team checks the requirements of each student's target universities.

How are science practicals assessed in an online programme?

In Pearson Edexcel International A Level Biology and Chemistry, practical skills are assessed through written papers (Units 3 and 6). These test experimental design, data analysis, error analysis and evaluation. ALMA students build these skills online, so they don't need to attend a separate laboratory exam.

When do ALMA students apply to medical school?

Students sit their IAS exams in the January 2028 series and apply with predicted scores built on those results. Applications for September 2028 intakes open around January 2028, and applications for Spring 2029 intakes open around June 2028. Exact deadlines vary by university, so we plan each student's timeline individually.

When does ALMA start and finish?

The first cohort starts in January 2027. Final IA2 exams are in the May/June 2028 series, which completes the programme.

Who can join ALMA directly?

O Level finishers and Grade 11 finishers from the government system can join ALMA directly (Track A). All applicants take the Diagnostic Placement Exam and a Structured Admissions Interview.

I have finished Grade 10 or the GED. Can I join ALMA?

Yes, through Track B. If you reach the passing score in the Diagnostic Placement Exam and interview, you join ALMA directly. If you don't yet reach it, you first complete our 6-month Pre-A Level Bridging Course, which covers quantitative chemistry, cell and physiology foundations, and algebra and functions.

What is the Diagnostic Placement Exam?

It is a placement test in scientific and mathematical reasoning, taken together with a Structured Admissions Interview. It shows whether a student is ready for ALMA's pace, or would benefit from the Pre-A Level Bridging Course first.

Is ALMA fully online?

Yes. All teaching is delivered live online by subject specialists, so students can study from anywhere in Southeast Asia and beyond.

Where do ALMA students sit their Pearson exams?

ALMA is taught online, and students sit the official Pearson exams in person at an exam centre. In Myanmar, exams are taken at centres assigned by the British Council and at Pearson centres. In Thailand, exams are taken at the Pearson centre in Bangkok.

Does ALMA include medical school admissions support?

Yes. Students get help with university shortlisting, personal statements, admissions tests such as UCAT where their target universities require them, and interview and Multiple Mini Interview (MMI) practice.

Are Pearson Edexcel International A Levels recognised by universities?

Pearson Edexcel International A Levels are accepted by universities around the world as an equivalent of UK A Levels. Entry requirements differ by university and programme, so students should always check the specific grades and subjects each university asks for. Our admissions team helps with this.

How do I apply for ALMA?

Register for the Diagnostic Placement Exam and Structured Admissions Interview through our pre-registration form, or contact our admissions team. Places in the January 2027 cohort are limited.
